# Public-source check, 20 October 2026

The [official League page](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/amd-lablab-ai-academy-challenge) still lists Match 4 as Intelligent Web Information Retrieval, live 13-27 October. Its current Match 4 detail link does not expose a separate task-specific brief. This check does not establish a cutoff timezone or a private evaluator schema.

AMD's [AI Developer Hub](https://www.amd.com/en/developer/resources/rocm-hub/ai-cloud-development.html), [container documentation](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/en/latest/install/docker-containers.html) and [PyTorch LLM playbook](https://developer.amd.com/playbooks/pytorch-rocm-llms/) remain useful primary deployment references. Latest documentation may describe a newer ROCm release. Aperture retains the exact organizer-mandated ROCm 10.0 image and digest rather than upgrading the base to match a moving `latest` page. General AMD documentation does not confirm the Match 4 adapter.

Public Match 4 entries checked include [Pinpoint](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/amd-lablab-ai-academy-challenge/innova-ottawa/pinpoint-exact-web-answers-with-their-source), [WebScout Exact](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/amd-lablab-ai-academy-challenge/vadym-bilobrovets/webscout-exact-match-4-retrieval-agent), [Kaminari](https://github.com/kaminariouji/kaminari-web-retrieval) and [ArcOmega WebRetriever](https://github.com/dcain2336/amd-league-match4-webretriever). Their descriptions support natural-language questions, optional starting URLs, evidence/source output and bounded navigation as **CONFIRMED_OBSERVED** behavior. They are third-party implementations, not organizer confirmation.

Pinpoint publicly reports 11/15 on AMD W7900D; WebScout reports 7/8 on its own set. Different datasets and self-reported measurements are not interchangeable. Aperture's reproduction of Pinpoint's visible questions uses the published loose answer-only scorer, with the scoring and hardware limitations documented in [BENCHMARKS.md](../BENCHMARKS.md). None of these public results is treated as a hidden-evaluator score or a ceiling on engineering effort.
